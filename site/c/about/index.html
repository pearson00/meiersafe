About Mike Meier

In brief

Mike Meier has been test flying hang gliders since 1976: experimental, developmental and production test flying of the gliders Wills Wing designs and builds. He has been a member of the Society of Experimental Test Pilots since 1992.

Test flying #

Mike started production test flying in 1976 and has flown experimental, developmental and production tests on Wills Wing's hang gliders ever since: flow separation studies, maximum roll rate tests, spin testing, and the final quality-control flight on gliders coming off the line.

SETP #

Below: from the 2018 Gulfstream talk, not in the paper.

Mike first got involved with SETP in 1992, co-presenting a paper with Mark Stucky at the national symposium on applying classical flight test techniques to a modern high-performance hang glider. He applied for membership afterwards, on the strength of his experimental test work in hang gliders.

By accepting my membership, the Society had, in effect, called my bluff. … And I had nothing. Luckily, within about a year of coming to this realization, I had the good fortune to nearly kill myself while landing a hang glider.
