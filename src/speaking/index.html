Speaking: where Mike has presented the paper

In brief

Mike first presented Lessons Learned and Murphy's Corollary at the SETP West Coast Section Symposium in March 2015, and gave it fifteen times by November 2018, to test pilots, hang glider pilots and aerospace companies. In May 2024 he discussed it with Art Tomassetti on the Flight Test Safety Committee podcast.

Two versions of the talk #

The Anaheim talk ran 25 minutes, followed by questions. The paper follows its form, with material that didn't fit the time added in indented italics: The paper.

The 45-minute version, given at Gulfstream Aerospace in January 2018, adds the 2015 certification launch and the hard questions, among them "Is 100% safety possible?"

The podcast #

Mike discussed the paper with Art Tomassetti on the Flight Test Safety Committee podcast, episode 54, "Go or No Go" (May 2024). The committee's podcast page lists the paper against the episode.
